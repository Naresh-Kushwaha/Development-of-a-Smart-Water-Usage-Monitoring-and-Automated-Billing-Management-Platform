# AquaTrack help centre - residents

## What the platform does
AquaTrack tracks the water each flat uses, builds the monthly water bill fairly, warns about leaks and overuse, and lets residents see their usage and invoices.

## Signing up and signing in
- Resident sign-up needs the apartment code (the admin sees it in their sidebar) and your unit number. The admin must have registered your unit first.
- If "Continue with Google" is shown you can use it instead of a password. The first time, you are asked for the apartment code and unit number to finish creating the account.
- Sign out from the sidebar. On the Profile page you can change your name and password (at least 8 characters).
- After many sign-in attempts in a minute you are asked to wait one minute.

## Dashboard
- Tiles: latest reading, this month's usage against the apartment's alert threshold, usage in the current billing cycle, and a bill preview.
- The bar under the tiles turns amber at 80 per cent of the monthly threshold and red above 100 per cent.
- Charts: daily litres for the last 30 days and monthly kilolitres (kL) for the last six months, with the alert line.
- The bill preview is an estimate for the open cycle. The final invoice is created only when the admin finalizes the cycle.
- A few water-saving tips are shown and change every day.

## Logging a reading
- Dashboard > "Log today's meter usage". Enter the date and the litres used on that day (daily litres, not the meter dial number).
- One reading per day per unit. A date in the future is not allowed. A day that already has a reading is rejected.
- Flats without a water meter cannot log readings and are billed by flat area. The admin can also upload readings in bulk.

## Bills and invoices
- The admin opens a billing cycle (a period such as one month). While it is open you see a bill preview. When the admin finalizes it, invoices are created and residents are notified by email.
- An invoice = water charge + your share of shared areas (garden, pool, lobby) + any adjustment made by the admin. Adjustments can be a credit (negative) or an extra charge.
- Two billing modes exist, chosen by the admin:
  - Tiered tariff: the first block of kL is charged at the base rate, usage above the limit at a higher rate, plus an optional fixed charge. Using more than the tier limit makes each extra kL cost more.
  - Cost sharing: the apartment's actual water purchase cost (tankers, municipal supply) is split in proportion to each flat's consumption. Flats without meters are estimated from their area.
- Shared-area water is split between flats by flat size or by number of occupants, as the admin chose.
- Reasons a bill can be high: more kL than the tier limit, a leak, a larger share of shared areas, or an adjustment on the invoice.
- Invoice status is UNPAID or PAID. Use the PDF button on the Invoices page for an itemised copy with payment instructions.
- Paying online: if online payment is enabled, an UNPAID invoice shows a "Pay now" button on the Invoices page. It opens Razorpay (cards, UPI, net banking). After a successful payment the status becomes PAID and a receipt is emailed. If there is no button, pay as described on the invoice PDF; the admin can mark it paid.

## Alerts
- Overuse alert: sent once a month when your usage this month passes the apartment's threshold. You get an in-app notification and an email with tips.
- Possible-leak alert: when your latest daily reading is far above your own recent average (needs about a week of readings). Check taps, toilet cisterns and pipes.
- All alerts are listed on the Alerts page.

## Compare page
- Shows your average daily litres for the last 30 days against similar-sized flats (area within 20 per cent of yours) and the apartment average, plus litres per person and a rank. Other households are anonymous.

## Water-saving tips
- Fix dripping taps; one drip per second wastes thousands of litres a year.
- Shorter showers; switch the tap off while brushing teeth.
- Run washing machines and dishwashers only with full loads.
- Test the toilet cistern for silent leaks with a drop of food colouring.
- Water plants early or late in the day with a bucket instead of a hose.

## What to ask the admin
Tariff rates, meter or unit details, wrong readings, billing cycle dates and invoice adjustments are managed by the apartment admin.
